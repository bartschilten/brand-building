A film strip with sprocket holes and frame numbers (K3) for campaign images on Home and Brand. `<FilmStrip frames={[{src, alt, label}]} caption="..." start={1} />`. A frame without `src` shows a visible placeholder (`[CAMPAIGN PHOTO 01]`). Frames are 3:2, cropped to cover; on a phone the strip scrolls sideways with snap. Film grain belongs in the photographs, not as a layer. Only campaign images, never packshots.
