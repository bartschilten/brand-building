The hero with the streetlight (B2), chosen by Bart, now a component. `<LanternHero eyebrow title intro>{buttons}</LanternHero>`. A 9 s cycle: dark, the light flickers on like a real lamp, the cone and glow rise, the asphalt texture appears in the lit patch and the headline lights up, then it flickers off. A click on the hero (not on a button) cycles automatic, always on, always off. Home and campaign pages only. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the light stays on without animation. Text stays readable in the dark phase (35 % opacity, then full). On a phone the lamp moves to the lower right so the light never sits behind the copy. `texture` overrides the lit surface (default asphalt).
