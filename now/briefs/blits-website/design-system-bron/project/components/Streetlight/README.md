The streetlight (B2) as a section: a lamp post in front of a quiet brick wall at night. On a 9 s cycle the lamp flickers on, the wall around it warms up and the content (photos, a film strip) gets lit through a soft light layer; then it goes out and the content stays visible but dimmer. A click on the section (not on a control) cycles automatic, always on, always off. `<Streetlight side="right|left">…</Streetlight>`. Use once per page at most, on Home or a campaign page, around imagery rather than running text. The wall is a drawn brick pattern with the concrete photo as grain; replace it with a real brick photo once there is one. Reduced motion: the light stays on.
