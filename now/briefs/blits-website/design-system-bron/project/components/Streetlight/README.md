The streetlight (B2) as a section: a lamp post in front of a quiet brick wall at night. On a 9 s cycle the lamp flickers on, the wall around it warms up and the content (photos, a film strip) gets lit through a soft light layer; then it goes out and the content stays visible but dimmer. A small switch next to the lamp ("LIGHT · AUTO / ON / OFF", a real button with `aria-pressed`, 44px target) cycles automatic, always on, always off. In automatic mode the cycle starts from the dark phase each time the section scrolls into view and stops when it leaves. The flicker is gentle (two dips, at least 340 ms apart). The dim layer is capped at 30 % and sits under headlines (give them `position:relative; z-index:4`). `<Streetlight side="right|left">…</Streetlight>`. Use once per page at most, on Home or a campaign page, around imagery rather than running text. The wall is a drawn brick pattern with the concrete photo as grain; replace it with a real brick photo once there is one. Reduced motion: the light stays on.
